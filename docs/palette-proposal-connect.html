Why this is not dark mode. The plan originally specified dark-dominant for Connect. Its stylesheet has 33 hardcoded colours, including body { background: #fff }, and its .dark bands assume a light page with dark sections. Inverting that is a rework rather than a token swap, and the result would have to be judged by eye. Light-cool gets the same separation with far less risk.
What still has to be your call. Whether this looks right. A machine can check that no two properties share a hex or a typeface. It cannot check whether the result is good. If the right hand column reads as a different, better product, say so and it gets applied to Connect and Business OS together.
